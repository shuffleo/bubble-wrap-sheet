Bubble Wrap: an interactive sheet to click, tap, and pop

Click or tap a bubble to pop it. Tap popped bubbles again to crinkle them without counting another pop. Drag any sheet corner to bend the sheet; arrow keys bend the focused corner. Hold and drag to pop a trail. Multiple fingers work together. The four QWERTY keyboard rows pop one random unpopped bubble per key press. Space refreshes the sheet. Enter on the focused sheet pops the next bubble. Held keys do not auto-repeat. Append ?panel=show to the URL for material controls and the key map and optional labels. Choose XS, S, M, L or XL in material controls to start a new sheet at that size. Cut bubbles at the boundary stay flat and are not included in the counter.

The material needs WebGL 2.

Enable hardware acceleration or try a current browser.

Material controls

Adjust the material, cut, and feel. Drag a corner to bend the sheet. Tap popped bubbles again to crinkle them.

Changes save on this device. Seeds refresh automatically.

Sound effects by Anthousai on Freesound under CC0.Three pop recordings and the refresh sound are bundled. Procedural crinkles remain a separate fallback.
